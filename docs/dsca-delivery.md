## The burst finding (the honest open item)

The 25m burst window (1,000 instances, churn continuous + the four burst shapes) FAILED its acceptance — and that failure is a deliverable: **the burst delete leg exposes a real product race**. Mechanism (verified from the child log across two review rounds): the 60s full-push tick's source snapshot is read before a burst's delete events arrive; its population-scale register wave lands after the event-path deregister completes — a register-after-deregister ghost healed only by the NEXT 60s reconcile tick (case-3, verified by the servIns-shaped rsync), which equals OBS_BOUND, so phase collisions breach (3 delete legs + 1 up leg past bound; max heal 60.01s). The coalescing queue is exonerated. The 2h window's zero divergence is phase commensurability (20s churn vs 60s wave, phase-locked non-overlap) + event-stream width, not pod age. **Fix design for the next batch:** a sink-side tombstone (short TTL on deregistered composite ids) and/or push-execution revalidation (the provider re-reads its cache inside the submitted closure) — either collapses the delete-leg convergence to the event-path tail (sub-second).
